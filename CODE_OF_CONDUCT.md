# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to making participation in Household AI respectful and
harassment-free.

## Expected behaviour

Participants are expected to:

- be respectful and constructive;
- keep disagreement focused on ideas, code, evidence, and project direction;
- accept feedback gracefully;
- respect personal boundaries, privacy, and maintainer time; and
- take particular care when discussing household data or safety-sensitive
  configurations.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Unacceptable behaviour includes:

- harassment, intimidation, threats, or personal attacks;
- discriminatory language or conduct;
- sexualised language or unwanted sexual attention;
- publishing another person's private information without permission; and
- sustained disruption of project discussions.

Maintainers may warn, restrict, or remove participants whose conduct is harmful
to the community.

## Reporting and scope

Reports should be made through the project's maintainer channels. Do not put
sensitive personal information in public issues or discussions.

This code applies in project spaces, including issues, pull requests,
discussions, documentation, chat, and project events.

This policy is adapted from common open-source community standards, including
the Contributor Covenant.
